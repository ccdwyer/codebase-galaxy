# Privacy

It runs entirely on your machine. It sends nothing over the network. It lists your repo's folders and reads up to a few hundred source files (each under 200 KB) through Claude Code's file access, only to find import links between them. It runs no programs. Clicking a star copies that file's path to your clipboard.

The mod collects no analytics or telemetry, and its author receives no data from it.
